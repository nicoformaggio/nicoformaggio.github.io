About

Hello, I am Nico. I am a Games Programmer and Designer but my main area of work is in Technical Design. I got into games very early into my childhood with my first game console bring the Nintendo 64. Playing games from a very early age really got me excited and passionate about the world of video games and it was at a young age (around 7) that I knew I wanted to work in the industry of games.

While I have been using computers and learning IT skills from a very young age it was at 15 that I started to learn programming and getting more into software and hardware with regards to IT work. I started properly learning code at Aylesbury College where I learnt C# code to tackle various problems and solve them by utilising problem solving skills alongside C# skills. I then made my first game Bucky-Ball in the 2nd year of College and passed my course with a triple distinction.

I now study at Staffordshire University on the Computer Games Design and Programming course where I am on my third and final year. Below are many examples of my work at University, some being group projects and they are labeled accordingly to what engine I used and any programming languages I used. I also designed and created this website using Bootstrap

If you have any questions. Feel free to contact me via the contact me section and I will be sure to get back to you as soon as possible :)
